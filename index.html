About

Who we are

Mila Healthcare AI RG is a reading group at Mila and McGill bringing together students, researchers, and clinicians to discuss machine learning for healthcare - from foundational methods to real clinical deployment.

We host talks from invited speakers, read and debate papers, and build a community around responsible AI in medicine.

Want to attend or give a talk? Send a quick request and we will get back to you.
